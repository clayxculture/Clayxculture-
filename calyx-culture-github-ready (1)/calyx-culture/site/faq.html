Questions, answered

Delivery, Cash on Delivery, sizes and exchanges.

Is Cash on Delivery available?

Yes. You can pay Cash on Delivery, or send your order on WhatsApp and pay when it arrives.

How long does delivery take?

Our team confirms the delivery time and shipping charge for your pincode on WhatsApp. Shipping is free once your bag crosses the free-shipping amount shown in the bag.

What is the return or exchange policy?

We do not accept returns. We exchange only verified defects, wrong items, transit damage or missing items reported within 3 days of delivery with an unboxing video. See the Policies page for details.

Which size should I pick?

We stock S, M, L and XL. Most styles are relaxed or oversized. Send your height and weight on WhatsApp and we will recommend a size.
