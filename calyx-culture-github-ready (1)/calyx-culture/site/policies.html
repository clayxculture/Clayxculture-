Returns, Exchange and Refund Policy

In short.
  • We do not accept returns. We do not exchange for change of mind, size, fit, colour or style.
  • We exchange only when we verify one of four problems: wrong item, manufacturing defect visible on delivery, damage in transit, or missing item.
  • Report within 3 days of delivery with an unboxing video.
  • If a replacement is not available, we refund.

1. What we cover

We will exchange an item, at our cost, if we verify any of these:

  1. Wrong item: a different product, size or colour from the one you ordered.
  2. Manufacturing defect visible on delivery: for example open stitching, a hole or tear, a broken zip or button, a faulty print, or a stain that came with the item.
  3. Damage in transit: the item or its packaging was damaged on the way to you.
  4. Missing item: an item or quantity listed on your order is not in the parcel.

2. What we do not cover

  1. Change of mind, or you simply do not like the item.
  2. Size, fit or length preferences. Please check the size details on the product page before ordering.
  3. Small colour differences caused by your screen, and size differences within 1 inch (2.5 cm) of the listed measurement.
  4. Items that have been worn, washed, altered, dry-cleaned, scented or used, or that are missing tags, original packaging or any free gift.
  5. Damage caused after delivery: misuse, wrong washing, pets, accidents.
  6. Natural characteristics of the fabric or wash, for example the faded look of washed denim, slight fabric variation, or loose threads that can be trimmed.
  7. Claims made after the time limit below, except hidden defects under section 9.

3. How to report a problem

  1. Record an unboxing video before you open the parcel, in one unbroken take. Show the parcel label and the sealed packaging, then the opening, then every item you received.
  2. Message us on WhatsApp +91 88991 90631 or email clayxculture@gmail.com within 3 days (72 hours) of the delivery date shown by the courier.
  3. Send your order ID, the phone number used for the order, the unboxing video, and clear photos of the problem and of the tag.
  4. We give you a complaint ticket number and keep your evidence on record.

If you do not have an unboxing video, you can still send clear photos of the parcel, the label and the problem, and we will assess them reasonably. A video makes approval much faster and is strongly recommended.

4. How we verify

We compare your evidence with the order details and with the packing record we keep for each order. We may ask for more photos or a short video, and we may ask you to return the item for inspection. Please keep the item, tags and packaging unused until we decide.

5. Decisions and timelines

  1. We acknowledge your report within 48 hours.
  2. We decide within 3 working days after we have your complete evidence, and we tell you our decision and our reason in writing.
  3. Your complaint is closed within one month at the latest.

6. If approved: exchange

  1. We arrange a pickup of the item at our cost. Please hand it over unused, with tags and in the original packaging.
  2. We ship the replacement of the same item, size and colour after the pickup is confirmed. We pay the shipping.
  3. Each item is exchanged once. The replacement is checked the same way.

7. If a replacement is not available: refund

If we cannot supply the same item, size and colour, we refund the price you paid for that item and, if the whole order is affected, the shipping you paid. Refunds go to your original payment method. For COD orders we refund by UPI or bank transfer to details you confirm in writing. Timeline: 5 to 7 working days after approval. By agreement, you may keep the item for a partial refund instead.

8. If your claim is not approved

We explain why, with reference to your evidence. You can ask the Grievance Officer (see Grievance Redressal) to review it, and you can always approach a consumer commission. We do not hold items or money without a reason.

9. Hidden defects

A defect that could not reasonably be seen on delivery, but shows during normal first use (for example a seam failing at normal wear), must be reported within 30 days of delivery with photos and the order ID. If we verify it as a manufacturing defect, the same exchange or refund applies.

10. False or abusive claims

We record claims to protect honest customers. Claims that we find to be false, altered or repeated without basis may lead to refusal of the claim, restriction of Cash on Delivery, closing of future orders and, where needed, legal action.

11. Your legal rights

This policy does not take away any right you have under Indian law, including rights for defective goods and unfair trade practices. Where the law gives you a remedy that this policy does not, the law applies.

  2. Once an order is dispatched, it cannot be cancelled. If you refuse a parcel without a problem covered by our Returns, Exchange and Refund Policy, it returns to us and no exchange or refund applies for that reason.
  3. Prepaid cancellations are refunded in full to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. We do not charge a cancellation fee.
  4. We may cancel an order, with a full refund, if an item is out of stock, a price or listing error occurred, the pincode cannot be served, or we cannot verify the order details. We will tell you before or soon after.

a retail store (seller only, not the manufacturer or supplier), trading as calyxculture, Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir. Contact for privacy requests: clayxculture@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  2. Messages: WhatsApp messages you send us, chat and contact messages on the site, and complaint evidence such as photos and unboxing videos.

  4. Technical and safety data: order frequency, address and phone patterns, used to detect fake orders and misuse. If we add a bot check such as Cloudflare Turnstile, it will also collect signals to tell humans from bots, and we will say so here.

3. Why we use it

  1. To take, confirm, pack, deliver, track, exchange and refund your order.
  2. To contact you about your order, on WhatsApp, phone or email.
  3. To prevent fraud and misuse, and to protect honest customers.
  4. To handle complaints, reviews and legal claims.
  5. To keep accounts and tax records and meet legal duties.

We use your data with your consent and for the purposes of the order you asked for, and where the law allows or requires it. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who we share it with

  1. Courier partners (the courier assigned to your pincode): your name, phone and address, to deliver.

After that we delete or anonymise the data, unless the law requires us to keep it.

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, erase it (except what we must keep by law), withdraw your consent, or name a person to act for you.
