Our story

Rooted in Srinagar. Made to move with you.

calyxculture is a streetwear label from Srinagar, Kashmir. We make oversized tees, baggy denim, hoodies and winter layers with one idea: clothes that feel good, look sharp and are easy to wear every day.

We are building a fashion and streetwear brand that stands for individuality, comfort and quality, and keeps great style within everyone’s reach.
